# Horizon-A-Geosphere-Museum
Horizons is an interactive VR game that explores three main topics: ocean technologies, human history, and the future of space exploration.

Purpose: Horizon is a three floor virtually reality museum built on one key factor and that is what if a museum was interactive. Instead of you getting told what life is like your living through it. Visitors will get to go under the sea and learn about the technology of ocean discovery and the fore front of innovation, go through over 1,000 years of history in a floor through towns and villages, and go soar through the galaxy with an adventure to mars and the moon. If it’s touching a turtle, standing inside of an egyptian village, or landing onto a mars base are simulation shows that Vr can be a legitimate educational tool, by replacing passive oveservation with hands on discovery

Intended Audience: The primary audience is middle and high school students (12-17) studing marine biology, world history, religion, or astronomy. Horizon can function as a field trip to places that would be inaccessible. Educators are a close second, using individual floors as curriculum supplements without added lesson planning. Overall museum visitors of all ages can have fun while learning about topics though touch and not just reading
